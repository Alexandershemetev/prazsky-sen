Programme: from A0 to B2

Classes take place twice a week for 80 minutes in convenient districts of Yerevan (Kentron, Nor Nork, Arabkir, Shengavit) or online.

Module 1 · Level A1

Live speech and clear pronunciation from the first lessons

Correct Czech pronunciation without an accent, overcoming the language barrier, and confident communication on public transport, in cafés, at the bank and on a student campus.

Module 2 · Level A2

Grammar that makes sense, without rote learning

The logic of Czech cases and verbs, built on shared Slavic roots, reading real articles and confidently understanding native speakers when they talk fast.

Module 3 · Level B1

Fluency and life in a Czech environment

Spontaneous speech, taking part in discussions and arguing your point of view. Watching Czech media, reading the press, and handling everyday and student tasks in Prague.

Module 4 · Level B2

Academic Czech for university admission

Subject-specific vocabulary for your field (economics, IT, medicine, engineering and more), taking lecture notes and writing essays.
